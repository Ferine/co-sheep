The sheep's diary

    This is the pasture

    Open this artifact in the Claude desktop app and choose Install as app: the flock then moves onto your real desktop.

    • Drag a sheep. Let go up high and it parachutes.
    • Double-click to poke. Hover two seconds to pet.
    • Right-click the white sheep to chat.
    • Drop one sheep on another to stack them.
    • Shake the mouse hard for a stampede.
